Terms of Use

Information about the scope and limitations of this personal research automation.

Personal, non-public tool

BETPILOT BUCO NERO is operated solely by its owner for data collection, storage and quality-control research. No public account access, managed service or availability guarantee is offered.

Accuracy and availability

Input match observations and third-party exports can be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate. Historical database entries are preserved with validation information, but no claim is made that they are suitable for predicting any sporting result or for financial decisions.

Google account access

The operator authorizes Google Drive access through the Google consent screen and can revoke it in their Google Account at any time. The broad Drive scope should be used only with appropriately protected OAuth credentials and reviewed periodically.

Third-party services

The automation depends on Google Drive, GitHub Actions, rclone, and separately obtained match exports. These providers have their own policies and service availability. The project is not endorsed by them.
